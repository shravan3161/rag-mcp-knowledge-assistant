Printer Model X Troubleshooting Guide

Problem: Printer is making a strange noise.

If the printer is making a strange noise, first check whether the paper feed mechanism is blocked.

Turn off the printer and inspect the paper feed area for any stuck paper or foreign objects.

If the problem continues after removing any obstruction, contact technical support.

Problem: Printer is not printing.

Check that the printer is powered on and connected correctly.

Make sure there is paper in the tray and that the ink or toner cartridge is installed correctly.
